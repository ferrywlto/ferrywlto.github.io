fear about layoff even they still saving file by clicking on visual studio menu rather than ctrl-S, never use CLI or built-in tools in their IDE to work faster. 

This is because our work enviornment protected us very well.

- The mediocre mouse based engineers cost so much for an enterprise. 
- Good engineers pre AI era will be 3x productive then before, and 10x productive than mediocre engineers.

They are weak and cannot solve problems alone before the AI era, depends on AI for everything in the AI era, never question but copy and paste whatever AI generated without the ability to explain what are they.
